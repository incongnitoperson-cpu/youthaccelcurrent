For teen founders

Young enough to be underestimated. Serious enough to build anyway.

Youth Accelerator is a global startup accelerator for teens and other young founders who are actively trying to turn an idea into a real company.

You can be early. You do not need funding, revenue, or a legal entity. You do need to be doing something concrete.

Apply as a founderSee the program

What “serious” looks like

Serious does not mean impressive on paper. It means action: talking to users, shipping a prototype, testing demand, getting a first customer, building technology, solving a distribution problem, or doing the unglamorous work required to move forward.

What founders get

  • 1:1 mentorship matched to the problem in front of the company
  • Introductions to operators, founders, investors, and specialists when useful
  • Founder events and opportunities to meet other ambitious builders
  • An international network rather than a local-only program

Not a business camp

If you are looking for a class that teaches entrepreneurship from scratch, there are better fits. Youth Accelerator is designed for people who want support around something they are already trying to build.

Common questions

Do I need to be incorporated?

No. Incorporation is not required.

Do I need revenue?

No. Founders can apply from idea validation through early revenue.

Can I join from outside the U.S.?

Yes. The program is global and has no location requirement.

If you searched for a startup accelerator for teens, startup incubator for teens, teen entrepreneurship program, startup mentorship for teenagers, or even a startup incubator for kids, the distinction is simple: Youth Accelerator is for young founders doing real work on real companies.
