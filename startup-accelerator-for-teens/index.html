For teen entrepreneurs

A startup accelerator for teens who are serious about building.

Youth Accelerator is a global startup accelerator for teens and young founders. We help ambitious builders get access to people who can actually help: founders, operators, investors, specialists, and mentors.

Our founders and mentors currently span 7 countries. The program is global, mentor-led, and designed around each founder's real company rather than a simulated business project.

Is this a startup incubator for kids?

Not in the classroom-camp sense. Youth Accelerator is for young people who are genuinely trying to build a startup or serious project. You can be early, but you should be taking action: interviewing users, building a product, finding customers, testing distribution, or otherwise moving the company forward.

What you can access

  • 1:1 startup mentorship for young founders
  • Introductions across an international startup network
  • Founder events, hackathons, and peer connections
  • Specialist and funding pathways when relevant
