For high school founders

A high school startup accelerator for people actually building.

Youth Accelerator is a global high school startup accelerator and young entrepreneur program built for founders who want real mentorship, introductions, and startup support—not just entrepreneurship classes.

Founders can apply from anywhere and at different stages, from idea validation to early revenue. Our founders and mentors currently span 7 countries, creating an international network for young builders.

More accelerator, less classroom

Most high school entrepreneurship programs focus on teaching the basics of business. Youth Accelerator starts with your actual company. Mentorship is matched around your product, market, growth challenges, fundraising questions, legal needs, or other bottlenecks.

Built for ambitious young founders

  • Direct startup mentorship
  • Connections to founders, operators, investors, and specialists
  • Founder events and hackathons
  • A global peer network of young entrepreneurs

If you are looking for a startup program for high school students, high school business incubator, startup mentorship for teens, or youth startup accelerator, Youth Accelerator is built around real startup progress.
