For high school founders

An accelerator for students who are already building.

Most high school entrepreneurship programs teach students how startups work. Youth Accelerator starts one step later: with the company you are actually trying to build.

Accepted founders get direct mentorship, targeted introductions, founder events, and access to an international network. You can be early. You just need to be taking the work seriously.

What makes it different

  • Your company is the curriculum. Support is shaped around the product, market, and bottleneck in front of you.
  • Mentors are matched for usefulness. A legal problem should lead to legal expertise; a distribution problem should lead to someone who understands distribution.
  • No location requirement. The network is international.
  • No fake startup required. We care about real progress, not a polished school project.

What counts as “serious”?

You do not need funding, a huge user base, or a registered corporation. Serious can mean interviewing users every week, shipping a first product, getting your first customers, testing distribution, building proprietary technology, or consistently working through a difficult problem.

What matters is that there is evidence of movement.

Who should apply

High school founders can apply from the idea stage through early revenue. Youth Accelerator is especially useful when you already know what you are building but need better access to people, expertise, or a startup network.

Common questions

Do I need revenue to apply?

No. Early-stage founders are welcome. Show us what you have actually done so far.

Is this a class or summer camp?

No. There is no simulated company. The work revolves around your real startup.

Can I apply from outside the U.S.?

Yes. Youth Accelerator is global and has no location requirement.
