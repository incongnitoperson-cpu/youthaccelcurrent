For student founders

A startup accelerator for students building real companies.

Youth Accelerator is a global student startup accelerator for ambitious young founders. We connect student entrepreneurs with experienced mentors, operators, investors, specialists, and other founders who can help them move faster.

Unlike a classroom entrepreneurship program, Youth Accelerator is built around what you are actually building. Founders can join at the idea, product, user, or early-revenue stage. Our founders and mentors currently span 7 countries.

What student founders get

  • 1:1 startup mentorship matched to the company and current challenge
  • Introductions across a global founder and mentor network
  • Startup events and founder-focused programming
  • Access to funding pathways and specialist support when useful

Who should apply

We look for serious student founders who are taking action. You do not need a famous school, funding round, or polished pitch deck. We care more about initiative, progress, and whether you are genuinely trying to build something valuable.

If you are searching for a startup incubator for students, student entrepreneur program, startup mentorship for students, or an accelerator for young founders, Youth Accelerator is designed for that use case.
