How the program works

  • Apply with what exists today. We care more about the work than the pitch.
  • Get matched around a real need. Mentorship should solve a problem, not fill a calendar.
  • Use targeted introductions. Operators, founders, investors, and specialists are useful when there is a reason to meet them.
  • Keep building. The goal is progress in the company, not completion of a course.

What stage should you be at?

There is no required stage. A founder can be validating an idea, building a first product, growing users, finding customers, or already generating early revenue. The common requirement is momentum.

Who is this for?

Students who are genuinely trying to build something and would benefit from better access. That can include high school founders, university founders, technical builders, solo founders, or small founding teams.

Common questions

Do I need to attend a specific school?

No. Youth Accelerator is independent of school and open globally.

What does a founder actually get?

Mentorship matched to the company, targeted introductions, founder events, and access to the broader network.

Do I need funding or revenue already?

No. Early-stage founders can apply, but the application should show real action and progress.
