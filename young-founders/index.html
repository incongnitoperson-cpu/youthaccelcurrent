For young founders

An accelerator for ambitious young founders.

Youth Accelerator helps young founders build faster through direct mentorship, startup connections, events, and access to a global network. Founders and mentors in the network currently span 7 countries.

We support founders at different stages, including idea validation, product development, early users, and early revenue. The point is not to collect credentials. The point is to build.

Startup mentorship shaped around the company

Instead of assigning the same curriculum to every founder, Youth Accelerator matches support to the problem in front of the company. That might mean go-to-market strategy, sales, legal or IP questions, fundraising, product, technical guidance, international expansion, or another specific challenge.

A global young entrepreneur network

Young founders can connect with peers, experienced entrepreneurs, operators, investors, and specialists across geographies. Events add an in-person layer to the network.

Whether you found us by searching for an accelerator for young founders, young entrepreneur accelerator, youth startup program, startup mentorship for young entrepreneurs, or global student startup network, the goal is the same: help serious young builders get access to people and resources that move their companies forward.
