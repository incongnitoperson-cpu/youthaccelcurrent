For young founders

Better access for people who are ready to build.

Youth Accelerator helps ambitious young founders get access to the people and relationships that are usually hardest to reach early: experienced mentors, operators, specialists, investors, and other builders.

The program is global, founder-specific, and open from idea validation through early revenue.

Support follows the bottleneck

A generic mentor is not automatically useful. We look at what the company needs now: sales, go-to-market, product, legal or IP, fundraising, technical decisions, international expansion, hiring, or something else.

The goal is to make the network practical rather than decorative.

What founders can access

  • 1:1 mentorship matched around the company
  • Targeted introductions across the startup ecosystem
  • Founder events and in-person opportunities
  • A peer network of ambitious young builders
  • Funding pathways for select companies when relevant

Who tends to fit

Founders who move quickly, ask good questions, follow through, and have evidence that they are working on something real. Credentials matter much less than initiative.

Common questions

What stage do I need to be at?

Anywhere from idea validation to early revenue. The important thing is that you are actively building.

How are mentors matched?

Based on the company, stage, and current challenge—not through a one-size-fits-all curriculum.

Is the program global?

Yes. There is no location requirement.
